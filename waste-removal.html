Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to bag or pile up the waste first?
No — you can if you like, but we're happy to do the loading ourselves. Just point us at it and we'll clear it.
What kind of garden waste do you take?
Grass cuttings, hedge and shrub trimmings, leaves, branches, old turf, soil, weeds and general green waste. If you've had landscaping done, we'll also shift the leftover rubble and hardcore.
Where does the waste go?
We dispose of everything responsibly, recycling green waste where possible rather than sending it all to landfill.
Can you do a one-off dump run?
Yes — whether it's a single trailer load or a full day of clearing and multiple runs, just tell us roughly how much there is and we'll quote it.
Do you remove waste after your own garden jobs?
Always — clearance and disposal are included as standard in our garden, tree and landscaping work. This page is for when you need waste taken away as a job on its own.
